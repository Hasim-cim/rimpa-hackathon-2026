# RIMPA Global Hackathon 2026

**Challenge:** *How might we build information management know-how into AI so that organisations can
better recognise and manage bias, misinformation and disinformation?*

**Team:** Hasim (data engineer, builder), plus two tech consultants (storyteller, expert/research).

| When | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Mon 19 Oct, 07:00–17:00 | OpenText, L6, 80 Pacific Hwy, North Sydney | Build day. **Mentor pitch at 15:00** |
| Tue 20 Oct, 08:00 | Royal Randwick Racecourse | **5-minute pitch plus 5 minutes of Q&A from 10:00** |
| Wed 21 Oct, 16:00 / 18:30 | RIMPA Live stage / Diamond Ball (black tie) | Finalists and winner |

Prize: $10,000 AUD · Sponsor: National Archives of Australia · Judging: Idea & Solution, Fit,
Functionality, Presentation & Team.
