**Next:**
1. Write the script against the real demo, beat by beat, with who speaks. Suggested spine:
   - **Hook (0:30):** the Deloitte fabricated references, or the AI scribe letter. One sentence each.
   - **Problem in IM terms (0:45):** AI output used in business *is* a record (NAA, NSW, PROV, QLD), but
     AI tools aren't recordkeeping systems. Nothing decides what to keep, nothing records the model,
     nothing checks the evidence. Name the four characteristics (authenticity, reliability, integrity,
     usability).
   - **Who and the gap (0:45):** the information manager. Guidance says *what* to capture, nothing says
     *how*. And NAA has no agentic guidance yet.
   - **Demo (1:30):** Setup shows the IM wrote the rules → Check on the hardship summary: an AI sentence is
     green, a human sentence is red, a citation is not found → Blocked → the eligibility note is Ready
     and gets a passport → Recall: 14 records, 3 decision-informing → Disposal: 3 destroyed, 3 held.
   - **Why it holds (0:45):** NAA risk tiers, NSW metadata rule, QLD source rule, PROV oversight, the NAA
     agentic list; hash chain without blockchain; records stay correctable and disposable.
   - **Value and scale (0:30):** the gateway: every AI app and agent in the organisation feeds the same
     engine; one policy, one register, one recall. Money line: a targeted re-review of a few hundred
     records instead of everything or nothing.
   - **Close (0:15):** "Records management decides what to trust by evidence, not by whether AI touched
     it. The information manager writes the test."
2. Slides: problem, who, demo (live, with video backup), standards, scale, team. Every fact sourced to
   `research/` or `docs/background-analysis.md`; nothing tagged ⚠️ on a slide.
3. Printed passport handout for the judges (from 01).
